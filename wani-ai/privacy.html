Wani Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Wani Pro ("Wani", "we", "us") is a personal finance app for people in Malawi. It reads the transaction SMS your banks and mobile-money services send you, and shows where your money goes. This policy explains what we collect, why, who else sees it, how long we keep it, and your rights.

1. Who we are

Wani is run by NXT Packet Solutions, the controller responsible for your data. Contact us about anything in this policy at tech@nxtpacket.com.

2. SMS access (READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS)

  • With your permission, Wani reads SMS on your phone and listens for new ones, so your payments appear without typing them in.
  • Only messages from the banks and mobile-money services Wani supports are kept: NBS Bank, National Bank, Standard Bank, FDH Bank, First Capital Bank, Airtel Money and TNM Mpamba. These are recognised by their sender on your phone. Personal messages are skipped on the device and are never stored or sent.
  • On first use Wani imports the last 12 months of these messages, then new ones as they arrive.
  • You can withdraw SMS permission at any time in your phone's settings. Wani keeps working with payments you add by hand or paste into the chat.

3. What we collect

  • Account: your name and email address. Your login (a password or Google sign-in) is held by our sign-in provider, Clerk; we never see your password.
  • Payments from bank SMS: amount, date and time, payee or merchant, bank, the last digits of the account, the balance reported, and the transaction reference.
  • Original SMS text: kept to help fix misread payments. "Keep original SMS text" is on by default; switching it off, at sign-up or later in Settings, deletes the stored texts.
  • What you add: categories, corrections, budgets, your emergency fund, and chat messages.
  • Sign-in sessions: device type and IP address, to keep your account secure and let you sign out other devices.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, or messages from anyone who is not one of the senders above. We do not use advertising SDKs, and we never collect your phone's advertising identifier. Usage analytics are off unless you switch them on (see section 5).

4. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To provide the service you signed up for: sorting payments into categories, totals, insights, budgets and your emergency fund. Basis: performing our contract with you.
  • AI sorting and chat answers: only with your consent, which is off by default. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings; Wani then uses its own rules and nothing is sent to the AI service.
  • Keeping your account secure: for example limiting repeated sign-in attempts. Basis: our legitimate interest in protecting your account.

We never sell or rent your data, never use it for advertising, and never use it for credit scoring.

5. Who else sees it

  • Anthropic (United States), only if AI is on. For sorting it receives the payee name, the payment description with phone and account numbers masked, a typical amount, and your category names. Payees that are only a phone number are never sent. For chat it receives your question and the figures needed to answer it. For the weekly note and the payday wrap it receives that period’s totals by category. Under Anthropic's commercial terms this data is not used to train their models.
  • Clerk (United States). Runs sign-in. It holds your email address, name, password (in a form it cannot read back) or your Google sign-in, and records of the devices you sign in on. Deleting your Wani account deletes your Clerk login too.
  • Google (Firebase, United States). Delivers notifications to your phone (it receives a device token and the notification text, such as a daily total), and receives crash reports when the app fails (technical details of the error, never your payments). Only if you switch on usage analytics, it also receives which screens and features you use, with no amounts, payees, messages or account details. Advertising identifiers are never collected.
  • Our hosting provider stores the database and encrypted backups on our behalf, under contract.
  • Other users never see your data. When several people independently correct the same business payee (for example a supermarket) to the same category, Wani can use that category for everyone. This never includes names, amounts, or payees that are phone numbers.

Payee names in your SMS can identify other people (for example someone you sent money to). We protect them in the same way as your own data.

6. International transfers

Our servers, Anthropic and Google may be outside Malawi. Where data leaves Malawi, or the EU and UK for users there, we rely on contractual safeguards with our providers.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your data is kept while you have an account.
  • You can delete all your transactions, or your whole account, at any time in Settings. Account deletion erases your data at once.
  • Encrypted backups age out within 12 months. If a backup is ever restored, deleted accounts are erased again before the service restarts.
  • Records of AI processing batches are deleted after 30 days.

8. Your rights

Under the Malawi Data Protection Act 2024, and the GDPR where it applies, you can:

  • See and download a copy of your data: Settings → Download my data.
  • Correct it: change any payment's category or your settings in the app, or ask us to fix your name or email.
  • Erase it: Settings → Delete all transactions, or Delete account.
  • Object or withdraw consent: switch off AI or "Keep original SMS text" in Settings, or withdraw SMS permission in your phone's settings.

For anything else, email tech@nxtpacket.com. We reply within one month. You can also complain to the Malawi Communications Regulatory Authority (MACRA) or, in the EU or UK, to your data protection authority.

9. Security

  • Your sign-in is stored in your phone's secure keystore.
  • So Wani works offline, a copy of what you last saw is kept in the app's private storage on your phone for up to a week. It is deleted when you sign out.
  • Data travels over HTTPS, and backups are encrypted.
  • Every request is checked so that a person can only reach their own data.
  • If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell the regulator and, where the risk to you is high, tell you directly.

10. Children

Wani is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change applies. The date at the top shows the latest version.
